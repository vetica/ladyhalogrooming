Services

Care for every coat

From a fresh bath to a full breed-standard style, every service is tailored to your dog’s coat, size and comfort.

Bath & Brush

A thorough bath, blow-dry and brush-out to leave the coat clean, soft and tangle-free. Add what else is included.

Full Groom

Everything in the Bath & Brush, plus a haircut and style suited to your dog’s breed, coat and lifestyle. Add details.

Hand-Stripping

The traditional technique for wire-coated breeds that preserves natural coat texture and color.

Most pet parents choose a haircut because it’s quicker and costs less. Hand-stripping is offered for those who want it.

Nail Grinding & Trims

Nails are mostly finished with a grinder for smooth, rounded edges that are less likely to snag or split. Clipping is available as well.

Teeth Brushing

A gentle brushing to freshen breath and support everyday oral hygiene.

This is brushing only. It is not a substitute for a dental cleaning at your veterinarian.

De-shedding

Removes loose undercoat to cut down on shedding around the house and keep your dog comfortable.

Included automatically for coat types that need it, and built into the price.

Flea & Tick Upgrade Add-on

An upgrade to any bath or full groom with two specialty shampoos and a finishing spray.

Oral or topical treatments are applied only if you provide them.

Puppy Grooming

Gentle, positive first grooms that help puppies get used to baths, dryers, brushes and handling. Add a recommended starting age.

Specialty Breeds & Styles
